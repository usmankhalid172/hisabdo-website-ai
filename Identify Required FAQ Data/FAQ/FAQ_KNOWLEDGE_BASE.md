## General

### FAQ-001 — What is HisabDo?

**Answer:** HisabDo is a digital Khata and Ledger app for managing khata, udhar, expenses, receivables, customers and business records.

**Source:** Official HisabDo website; Google Play listing

**Verification:** Verified

**Answer:** It is designed for shopkeepers, retailers, wholesalers, freelancers, service providers, small businesses and people who want to track financial records.
