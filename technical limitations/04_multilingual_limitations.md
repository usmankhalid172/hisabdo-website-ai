# Multilingual Limitations

## Overview
Supporting users in multiple languages introduces additional technical challenges.

## Limitations
- The AI models are primarily optimized for English input.
- Urdu and Roman Urdu queries may not always be interpreted correctly.
- Mixed-language input can reduce classification accuracy.
- Financial terms may have different meanings across languages.
- Additional multilingual datasets are required.
- Language-specific model training may be necessary in future versions.

## Conclusion
Future versions should include multilingual datasets and language-aware AI models to improve accessibility.
