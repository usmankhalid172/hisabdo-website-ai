# Infrastructure and Cost Limitations

## Overview
Scaling AI-powered features requires additional infrastructure and financial resources.

## Limitations
- Cloud hosting costs can increase with more users.
- AI APIs may have usage-based pricing.
- More powerful servers may be required for large-scale AI processing.
- Database storage requirements will increase over time.
- Model training may require additional computational resources.
- API rate limits may restrict the number of requests.
- Continuous monitoring and maintenance can increase operational costs.

## Conclusion
A cost-effective and scalable infrastructure strategy is required for sustainable growth.
