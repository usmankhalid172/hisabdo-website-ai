# Model Limitations

## Overview
The AI and machine learning models used in HisabDo have limitations that can affect prediction accuracy and reliability.

## Limitations
- Model accuracy depends heavily on the quality of training data.
- Limited training data can cause incorrect predictions.
- The expense categorization model may struggle with unfamiliar expense descriptions.
- AI-generated responses can occasionally be inaccurate or irrelevant.
- Models may require retraining as user behavior and financial patterns change.
- Confidence scores do not guarantee that predictions are always correct.
- The system may have difficulty understanding highly complex or ambiguous queries.

## Conclusion
Continuous evaluation, improved datasets, and periodic retraining are necessary to improve model performance.
